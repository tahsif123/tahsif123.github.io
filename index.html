Research Interests

I am a final-year Mechanical Engineering student at BUET with seven papers and manuscripts across robotics, aerial systems, vehicle dynamics, thermal-fluid simulation and machine learning.

The common thread in my work is building physical systems and understanding them through experiments, simulation and data. I like taking a project from CAD and fabrication through to testing and a written paper.

What I bring to a lab

Hands-on design and fabrication (CAD, 3D printing, machining, assembly), experimental testing with sensors and actuators, and simulation across FEM, CFD and multibody dynamics. I have led a Mars rover sub-team and written four first-author papers and manuscripts as an undergraduate.

What I want to work on next

Adaptive robotic and electromechanical systems that sense and respond to their environment: reconfigurable and soft mechanisms, integrated sensing and actuation, and the fabrication methods that make them possible.

Research Experience

  1. 2025 – 20261st author · Presenter

    Additively Manufactured Morphing Quadcopter

    Department of Mechanical Engineering, BUET

    • Designed the frame in CAD and fabricated it by FDM 3D printing for a light, structurally efficient morphing mechanism.
    • Calibrated the servo-driven morphing mechanism and integrated it with the flight controller to switch between X, H and O configurations.
    • Validated the platform experimentally.

    OutcomeFirst-author paper presented at IEEE RAAICON 2026.

  2. 2025 – 20261st authorUnder review

    Mother-and-Daughter Drone System for Flood Search and Rescue

    Department of Mechanical Engineering, BUET

    • Designed a hybrid-powered mother drone that carries, deploys and recharges four battery daughter drones.
    • Built a mission simulator and compared the system with conventional search drones on synthetic flood maps and a real flood map from Sylhet (June 2022).

    OutcomeFirst-author paper submitted to ICEEICT 2027 (under review).

  3. 2026 – Present1st authorInternational collaboration

    Rollover-Safe Operating Limits of the Bangla Tesla Three-Wheeler

    Supervisor: Dr. Abdullah Al Amin, Assistant Professor, University of Dayton, USA

    • Built a CAD-derived multibody model of a battery-electric delta three-wheeler in PyChrono with a TMeasy-type tyre model.
    • Simulated braking, cornering, J-turn and lane-change manoeuvres across a range of speeds to find the rollover-safe steering limit at each speed.

    OutcomeFirst-author journal manuscript in preparation.

  4. 2026 – PresentB.Sc. Thesis

    Machine Learning Based Engine Diagnosis and Predictive Maintenance

    Supervisor: Dr. Md. Ehsan, Professor, Department of Mechanical Engineering, BUET

    • Logged real-time OBD-II engine data (ELM327, Torque Pro) and built a preprocessing pipeline that standardises columns and units and removes faulty readings.
    • Trained a Random Forest model for fault diagnosis and an Isolation Forest model, trained on normal driving only, for early warning.
    • Built an offline Streamlit app that explains each alert with root-cause sensors, related trouble codes and repair guidance.

    OutcomeB.Sc. thesis in progress, with a working diagnosis model and monitoring app.

  5. 20263rd authorUnder review

    Capability-Aware, Torque-Adaptive Robot Navigation

    Supervisor: Dr. Mohammad Mamun, Professor, BUET

    • Contributed to a ROS 2 framework in which RGB-D perception labels terrain as go, go-with-torque or no-go based on the wheel torque the robot can supply.
    • The planner and the per-wheel torque controller share this one capability estimate; tested on a differential-drive robot (Jetson Orin Nano, RealSense D435i) and in a physics-based simulator.

    OutcomeThird-author paper submitted to ICEEICT 2027 (under review).

  6. 2024 – 20257th authorBest Student Paper

    Multi-Purpose Autonomous Rover (Prochesta V3.0)

    Team Interplanetar, BUET Mars Rover Team · Supervisor: Dr. Kazi Arafat Rahman, BUET

    • Contributed as a member of the team that designed, built and field-tested the rover.
    • The rover has a four-wheel bar-differential suspension, a 5-DOF arm with 5 kg payload, a 30 cm drilling science module and a 5 km communication range.

    Outcome30.7% lighter than its predecessor at about $3,800. Published at ICME 2025 and won the Best Student Paper Award.

  7. 20261st authorIn preparation

    Mixed Convection in a Z-Shaped Vented Chamber

    Supervisor: Dr. Sumon Saha, Professor, BUET · CFDHT Research Group

    • Solved the steady laminar Navier–Stokes and energy equations by the Galerkin finite element method in COMSOL, after grid-independence and validation studies.
    • Varied the Richardson, Reynolds and Grashof numbers and cylinder size, and analysed their effect on flow and heat transfer.

    OutcomeFirst-author journal manuscript in preparation.

  8. 20264th authorUnder review

    Rack-and-Pinion Regenerative Suspension

    Supervisor: Dr. Md. Ehsan, Professor, BUET

    • Contributed to a mechanically rectified regenerative suspension built from additively manufactured and off-the-shelf parts, with a one-way clutch and DC generator.
    • Bench-tested the prototype charging a lithium-ion pack.

    OutcomeFourth-author paper submitted to ICEEICT 2027 (under review).
